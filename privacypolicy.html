Quick Summary

We process your data purely to deliver and improve our services. We never sell your personal contact records to third-party data brokers. You retain full control over your stored records at all times.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we gather include:

Provided Information

Account names, email addresses, phone numbers, and billing details submitted during registration or profile setup.

Automatic Logs

IP addresses, browser types, operating system details, time stamps, and referral paths captured seamlessly via site navigation.

2 How We Use Data

The data collected is utilized strictly under lawful business standards to ensure optimal operations:

  • Provision, maintenance, and ongoing enhancement of our digital application features.
  • Processing your direct transactions and transmitting necessary service invoices or notices.
  • Detecting, analyzing, and mitigating potential fraudulent activities or cyber security risks.

3 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our platform and hold certain analytical data. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

4 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not rent, sell, or trade personal data to third parties. We may share information only in specific strict scenarios:

Legal Obligations

When mandated by valid subpoenas or local governance courts.

Trusted Vendors

Cloud infrastructure hosts bound by secure data handling agreements.

Business Transfer

In the event of a merger, corporate acquisition, or asset sale.

5 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic residency (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you maintain specific rights regarding your personal records:

  • The right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you.
  • The right of rectification if any collected profile data is found to be inaccurate.
  • The right to object to or restrict processing operations of your personal details.
  • The right to data portability for secure transfer to another provider.

6 Security Measures

The security of your data is paramount to us. We deploy industry-standard SSL encryption protocols, robust firewall protections, and strict access hierarchies to guard your records against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.
